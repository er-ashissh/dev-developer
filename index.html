Journey of DevDeveloper  🎉

  • January 2012

    DevDeveloper was founded by Ashish K. Sondagar,
    The Company was established with the Vision of Transforming
    how Businesses engage with Customers Digitally.
    Initially, it started as a Software Service provider platform. 💼

  • March 2013

    The Company Ventured into e-commerce with
    the Launch of the DevDeveloper Marketplace,
    Offering a wide range of Products & Services. 💹

  • May 2015

    DevDeveloper started with a focus on the food & beverage industry,
    Offering a platform that allowed restaurants to manage orders,
    payments, & customer engagement seamlessly. 💸

  • February 2020

    Despite the challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic,
    DevDeveloper experienced rapid growth as businesses
    sought digital solutions to cope with the changing market dynamics.
    The platform helped many businesses transition to online ordering & payments. 😌

  • April 2023

    DevDeveloper continued to Grow its Customer Base & Expanded
    It's market presence across various cities in India.
    The company partnered with several large retail chains &
    Small Businesses, Providing them with Comprehensive Digital Solutions. 🤝
